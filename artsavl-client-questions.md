# ArtsAVL — Client Questions (RFP Q&A and Aug 11 Meeting)

30 questions from the assessment, split by whether they should be asked in writing or held for conversation. Full text in `engagements/artsavl-creative-portal/client-questions.md`. Context: [[artsavl-technical-assessment]].

## Two procedural points

**The RFP's question deadline is the same day as its proposal deadline.** §11 says questions are due "by the deadline listed above," and the only deadline listed is August 15 — the day proposals are due. Answers arriving that day cannot inform a proposal. Open by asking for an earlier response commitment; noticing it and handling it gracefully is itself a signal.

**Answers may be circulated to every bidder.** §11 reserves the right to distribute responses "to all known prospective firms." Every written question is therefore a public statement of how we think — free evidence of competence against the 20% understanding criterion, but also a transfer of our analysis to competitors. Hence the split below.

**The Aug 11 meeting recorded in [[artsavl-overview]] changes the routing.** Several questions are better asked live, where we can pair a challenge with a way to de-risk it cheaply. See [[artsavl-assessment-findings-vs-initial-read]].

## The questions that do the work

Ask these regardless of channel — each says, without claiming it, that we are modelling ArtsAVL as a business with a product to operate rather than a codebase to service.

- **Licensing operating model.** When a partner licenses the platform, does ArtsAVL **operate and host** that partner's instance — becoming the software provider partners pay for hosting, support and upgrades — or hand each partner a deployment they run? The single largest architectural fork, and it is a business decision.
- **Partner payment collection.** Would partner membership and advertising payments land in each partner's own bank account or ArtsAVL's with remittance? Largest cost variable in Goal 4, and nothing in the RFP addresses it.
- **What varies per partner.** What must stay identical across all partner instances and what must each partner control — branding, taxonomies and regions, membership pricing, moderation rules, email templates, page content? A shared taxonomy and a per-partner taxonomy are materially different builds.
- **Baselines.** Active member profiles and organization accounts; annual membership revenue and renewal rate; annual advertising revenue and active advertiser count; monthly visitors to directory, events and opportunities. Nothing can be sized without these.
- **Support history.** Support requests per month and hours per year the current arrangement consumes. Pricing a support agreement without that history means pricing a contingency into it, which serves neither side — and three finance professionals sit on the board.
- **Scope permission.** §11 invites recommendations for a different scope. Should proposals treat all six goals as in-scope, or is ArtsAVL open to fully delivering a prioritized subset with the remainder sequenced into a defined later phase?

Funding source and whether the six-month timeline is driven by a grant deadline · whether $250K requires additional board approval · whether membership pricing and tiers are open to change · how much staff time the email-based nonprofit package and ad sales consume · named partner organizations already in conversation and how many wanted live within twelve months · how partner support would be handled operationally · who consumes the existing JSON event API and what they cannot get · whether syndication is free distribution, a paid product, or both by partner type · **finalist repository read access under NDA** · availability of the outgoing partner for a paid transition period · **WCAG conformance level expected and whether an audit exists** · data volumes · which newsletter platform · who maintains WordPress and whether consolidation is in scope · existing brand and design standards and whether a design partner should be coordinated with · our primary counterpart and their weekly availability · whether a technical advisor participates in evaluation · fixed dates to design around (board meetings, State of the Arts Brunch, 2027 Arts Guide cycle opening in October, renewal periods) · **what Linkup and Rastrac do** · whether Little Green Light is the Goal 2 CRM and whether donors and members are linked · why listings are absent from the initial page response and whether profiles are individually indexable · who controls `ashevillearts.com` · metered service costs and whether per-partner infrastructure is absorbed or passed through · whether the no-build-step constraint is firm · subdomain vs custom partner domains.

## Hold back from writing

| Question | Why |
|---|---|
| Why is the current development partner transitioning out, and are they bidding? | Reads as distrust and puts Katie in an awkward position about a relationship we know nothing about. The delivery-relevant part — access to the outgoing team — is captured separately without the implication. **Good live question on Aug 11, framed as risk rather than blame.** |
| Does anyone at ArtsAVL have the background to evaluate a rebuild-vs-enhance recommendation? | The polite version gets the same answer. Asked directly it tells every competitor the evaluation is winnable on clarity rather than depth — which is our read and worth keeping. |
| Is the licensing ambition validated with peer councils, or an untested assumption? | The highest-value thing to raise, and **the strongest single item to bring to Aug 11** — in conversation we can pair the challenge with a cheap way to test it. In writing, distributed to competitors, it reads as questioning the executive director's strategy in front of an audience. |
| Who else is bidding? | Never lands well, rarely answered honestly. |
